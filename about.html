About MMJ Shuttles

MMJ Shuttles is a proudly South African transport service that provides reliable, affordable, and safe shuttle services for daily commutes, private events, and airport transfers. Founded with a passion for convenience and community, we aim to be your go-to ride partner across Gauteng and surrounding areas.

Our fleet includes comfortable Cars and Minibuses — all operated by experienced and friendly drivers. Whether you're heading to work, catching a flight, or organizing transport for your next big event, MMJ Shuttles has got you covered.

We value safety, punctuality, and great customer service. Ride with us once — and you'll never look back.
